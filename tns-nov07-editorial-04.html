Texas Activates Emergency Resources Ahead of Potential Weekend Flooding

State officials moved additional emergency resources into position ahead of a possible weekend flood threat.

On November 6, Texas officials announced the activation of additional emergency resources ahead of a potential weekend flood threat.

The action is a preparedness measure, not proof that widespread flooding has already occurred. Actual risk varies significantly by county, rainfall intensity and local terrain.

Residents should follow local National Weather Service alerts and avoid driving into flooded roadways. Low-water crossings can become dangerous even when nearby roads appear passable.

For Hill Country communities, the key questions are where the heaviest rain develops and whether rapid runoff affects creeks and low-water crossings.
