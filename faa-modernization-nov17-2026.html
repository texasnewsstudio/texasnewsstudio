FAA Outage Fallout Renews Calls for Modernization

The Federal Aviation Administration's explanation of a recent nationwide ground stop has sharpened questions about the resilience of air-traffic communications.

Latest reporting

In a November 15 account of the November 13 disruption, the FAA said unstable legacy network equipment was overwhelmed by connection attempts during a routine remote router replacement. The agency reported that the system was stabilized and traffic resumed at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on November 13.

Why it matters

The agency said it disabled implicated router functionality and took precautions on similar equipment. The incident is over, but it provides a concrete example of why modernization and redundancy remain important.
