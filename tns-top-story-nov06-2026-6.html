NORTH TEXAS · NOVEMBER 6, 2026

Cold Front Brings Breezy Day To Dallas-Fort Worth

National Weather Service expects cooler conditions Friday and a limited rain chance later in the weekend.

The National Weather Service in Fort Worth reports a cold front moving south across North Texas early Friday. Breezy north winds and cooler daytime readings follow. Light rain is possible in parts of Central Texas and far western North Texas Saturday night into early Monday, with generally limited amounts.
