DAN PATRICK OUSTED! TEXAS LIEUTENANT GOVERNOR RACE TURNS

Vikki Goodwin defeats Dan Patrick, changing the leadership of the Texas Senate.

Democrat Vikki Goodwin defeated Republican Lieutenant Governor Dan Patrick, Reuters reports, in one of the most consequential statewide results of the election.

The lieutenant governor presides over the Texas Senate and has substantial influence over committee assignments and the movement of legislation.

The outcome raises questions about how the next legislative session will address property taxes, school finance, electricity reliability and border security.
