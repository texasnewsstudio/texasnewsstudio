CANNONBALLS AT THE ALAMO BRING 1836 BACK INTO FOCUS

Archaeologists uncovered rare projectiles during work near the historic church.

TEXAS NEWS STUDIO · NEWS DESK

Archaeological work near the Alamo Church in San Antonio has uncovered cannonballs linked to the 1836 battle. The discoveries offer physical clues about a moment central to Texas history, although researchers still have questions about exactly how the projectiles were used. Even nearly two centuries later, careful excavation can reveal new evidence at one of the state’s most familiar landmarks.
