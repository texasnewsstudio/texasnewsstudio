TEXAS HAS A MAP OF HAUNTED PLACES AND UFO LEGENDS

TxDOT’s unusual Phantom Layer turns a road-trip map into a tour of Texas folklore.

TEXAS NEWS STUDIO · NEWS DESK

A transportation map may not be where you expect to find haunted hotels and UFO stories. But the Texas Department of Transportation added a Phantom Layer to its statewide planning map, featuring legends and spooky landmarks from across Texas. The entries are folklore and historical curiosities, not official confirmation of supernatural events. It is a creative way to explore lesser-known corners of the state.
