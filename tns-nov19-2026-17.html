ANTARCTICA ICE-CLIFF TWIST! 15,000 COLLAPSES REVEAL SURPRISE

New research challenges a straightforward doomsday picture of polar ice cliffs.

TNS editorial alert artwork; not a photograph of this incident.

Texas News Studio | November 19, 2026

A study highlighted November 19 analyzed more than 15,000 Antarctic ice cliffs and suggested some may be less vulnerable to runaway collapse than previously feared.

The research does not mean Antarctica's ice is safe or that sea-level rise has stopped. It instead refines how scientists model one particular mechanism of ice-sheet instability.
