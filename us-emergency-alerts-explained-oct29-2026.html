How U.S. Emergency Alerts Work: FCC, FEMA and the EAS Explained

What U.S. emergency warning infrastructure does—and what a test does not mean.

Editorial context: Emergency readiness advice, scheduled alert tests and routine military exercises are separate activities. None of the cited official sources establishes that a specific global catastrophe is imminent.

The U.S. warning network

The FCC regulates the Emergency Alert System, while FEMA’s Integrated Public Alert and Warning System supports authenticated emergency messages. Wireless Emergency Alerts can reach compatible phones.

Routine testing helps confirm that warnings can be delivered during real disasters. A test is not itself an emergency declaration.

Verify dates and alerts

Check FEMA and FCC before reporting an upcoming nationwide test. Specific dates should not be treated as confirmed without a current official notice.
