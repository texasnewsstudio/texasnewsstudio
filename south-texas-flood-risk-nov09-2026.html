South Texas Flood Risk Persists as More Rain Moves Through

Rain continues across South Central Texas Monday, November 9, with the National Weather Service warning that heavier pockets may cause localized flooding, especially near the Southern Edwards Plateau, Rio Grande Plains and the San Antonio-to-Coastal-Plains corridor.

NWS Austin/San Antonio says another 0.5 to 1.5 inches could fall over parts of the Southern Edwards Plateau, while a southern corridor could receive 1 to 3 inches with isolated higher totals. Several roads have already flooded in the Edwards Plateau area. Never drive into water covering a road; conditions can change quickly. Rain chances decrease into Tuesday, with the longest-lasting showers focused farther south.
