Hill Country Cold Snap Gives Way to Milder Weather

After a chilly start to the week, the Texas Hill Country is expected to warm gradually through midweek, according to the National Weather Service.

Latest reporting

The Austin/San Antonio forecast discussion for Tuesday, November 17 described below-average temperatures persisting Tuesday, with some Hill Country areas starting in the low 40s and isolated rural locations potentially cooler. Highs Tuesday and Wednesday were forecast generally in the 60s to lower 70s across South Central Texas.

Why it matters

Temperatures are expected to trend warmer later in the week. Rain chances remain low until late Saturday, and readers should check local forecasts for updated conditions.
