Target Air Fryer Recall: About 175,951 Gourmia Ovens Affected

Gourmia 9-slice digital air fryer ovens sold at Target are recalled because doors can unexpectedly close.

A recall announced in early November covers approximately 175,951 Gourmia stainless steel 9 Slice Digital Air Fryer Ovens sold at Target.

According to reporting on the consumer safety notice, the oven door can unexpectedly close when bumped while a person is removing hot food, creating a burn hazard.

Owners should compare their appliance’s model and identifying details with the official recall instructions before using it again. The recall does not cover every Gourmia appliance.

Kitchen appliances are frequently used in tight spaces, so a door that closes unexpectedly can create an immediate contact risk when hot trays are being handled.
