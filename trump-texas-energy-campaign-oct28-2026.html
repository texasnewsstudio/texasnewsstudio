Trump’s Texas Campaign Stops Put Energy and Data Centers in Spotlight

Original editorial illustration by Texas News Studio; not a photograph of the reported event.

President Donald Trump campaigned in Texas on October 27, backing Republican Senate candidate Ken Paxton ahead of the November 3 midterm elections, according to the Associated Press.

During the appearances, Trump discussed energy policy, including his intention to reverse Biden-era methane regulations, and encouraged communities to welcome data center development. Those are policy positions and proposals, not evidence that specific rules have already changed.

The campaign is unfolding amid disputes over energy costs, the Iran war and how rapidly growing computing facilities should be connected to the electric grid. Democrat James Talarico is contesting the Senate race.

Voters and businesses should watch for formal agency actions, published regulations and local development filings rather than assuming campaign statements immediately alter the law.
