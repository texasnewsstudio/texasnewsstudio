NATIONAL WEATHER | NOVEMBER 25, 2026

HOLIDAY TRAVEL TROUBLE! POWERFUL STORM TARGETS EAST COAST

Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Heavy rain, strong winds and coastal flooding threaten Thanksgiving travel, while mountain snow hits the Northwest.

NOAA forecasts an intensifying storm along the Eastern Seaboard on Thanksgiving, with heavy rain and strong winds from the Carolinas toward southern New England. Wet snow is possible in the middle Mississippi Valley, while another system brings rain and higher-elevation snow to the Pacific Northwest. Travelers should check airline notices and road conditions before departure. Delays are possible, not guaranteed.

Source: NOAA Thanksgiving week outlook, November 24; NWS Weather Prediction Center, November 25.
