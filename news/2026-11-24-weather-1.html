FREEZE WARNING! NORTH TEXAS BRACES FOR THANKSGIVING CHILL

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Parts of North Texas face freezing temperatures tonight, with a broader freeze possible Wednesday night.

The National Weather Service in Fort Worth expects scattered showers today, possibly mixed with a few sleet pellets, but no accumulation. Thanksgiving afternoon should be sunny with highs in the 50s. Forecasts may change.
