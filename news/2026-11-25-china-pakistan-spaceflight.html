SPACE FIRST! PAKISTANI ASTRONAUT JOINS CHINESE MISSION

Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

China launched its Shenzhou-24 mission with its first foreign astronaut.

Reuters identifies the international crew member as Pakistani, marking a milestone for cooperation in China's crewed space program.

Source: Reuters, via Arab News.
