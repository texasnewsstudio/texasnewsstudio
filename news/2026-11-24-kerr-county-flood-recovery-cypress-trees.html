HOPE AFTER THE FLOODS! KERR COUNTY REPLANTS GUADALUPE RIVER TREES

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Native cypress planting is helping restore river habitat after devastating Hill Country flooding.

The recovery effort combines ecological restoration with community healing. Replanting native trees is one part of the longer process of rebuilding the river corridor.

Source: Texas Tribune, November 24 news coverage.
