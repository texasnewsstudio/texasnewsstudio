TEXAS WEATHER | NOVEMBER 25, 2026

GULF COAST COLD BLAST! FREEZE WATCH FOR FIVE TEXAS COUNTIES

Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Temperatures could reach 30–32°F in parts of Southeast Texas by Thanksgiving morning.

The Houston/Galveston weather office has issued a freeze watch for Brazos, Burleson, Madison, Houston and Trinity counties overnight. Frost is possible and wind chills may dip into the 20s in some areas. Offshore winds and elevated seas are also a concern for small craft. Check updated local alerts before traveling.

Source: National Weather Service Houston/Galveston, November 25, 5:22 a.m. CST.
