HOUSTON COLD BLAST! THANKSGIVING MORNING COULD DROP INTO THE 30S

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

A cold front brings scattered showers and a sharp temperature drop to Southeast Texas.

Houston could see morning temperatures in the upper 30s around Thanksgiving, with colder conditions farther north. A widespread freeze is not expected in Houston itself, and temperatures should recover heading into the weekend. Forecasts may change.
