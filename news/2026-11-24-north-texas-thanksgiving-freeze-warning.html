THANKSGIVING FREEZE ALERT! NORTH TEXAS BRACES FOR BITTER MORNINGS

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Freeze warnings cover parts of North Texas tonight, with more widespread freezing temperatures expected Wednesday night.

The Fort Worth National Weather Service issued its Tuesday morning forecast at 5:08 a.m. CST. Light rain may mix with sleet or flurries in some communities today, but forecasters do not anticipate accumulation. Residents should protect exposed pipes, tender plants and pets. Thanksgiving is expected to begin a drier, warmer stretch.

Source: National Weather Service Fort Worth, area forecast discussion, Nov. 24.
