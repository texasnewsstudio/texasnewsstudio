WHITE HOUSE DIPLOMACY! QATAR LEADER MEETS TOP U.S. OFFICIALS

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Qatar's prime minister held separate meetings with JD Vance and Marco Rubio on Monday, according to AP reporting.

The November 24 Just Security briefing reports that the meetings took place at the White House. The source cited by AP did not disclose the substance of the discussions. No particular diplomatic agreement can be inferred from the meetings alone.

Source: Just Security Early Edition, Nov. 24, citing Associated Press.
