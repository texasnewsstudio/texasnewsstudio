THANKSGIVING FREEZE! TEXAS BRACES FOR ANOTHER ICY MORNING

Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

More widespread freezing temperatures are forecast tonight across North and Central Texas.

The National Weather Service in Fort Worth expects another cold night after Wednesday morning temperatures dipped into the upper 20s and lower 30s in parts of the region. Sunshine and afternoon highs in the 50s are expected on Thanksgiving. Protect sensitive plants and outdoor plumbing and provide warm shelter for pets. A warmer Friday and Saturday are forecast before another front Sunday.

Source: National Weather Service Fort Worth, November 25, 5:03 a.m. CST.
