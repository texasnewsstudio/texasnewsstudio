HOLIDAY AIRPORT CRUNCH! DFW BRACES FOR MILLIONS

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Dallas-area airports are preparing for heavy Thanksgiving traffic, with DFW expecting about 3.1 million passengers over its holiday period.

Axios Dallas reported that DFW anticipates a particularly busy Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday. Travelers can reduce last-minute stress by checking flight status, allowing extra time for security and arranging parking in advance. Passenger projections are estimates rather than final totals.

Source: Axios Dallas, Nov. 23.
