ARMY DETONATES LEAD EXPLOSIVES IN IOWA; HEALTH MONITORING UNDERWAY

Published Wednesday, November 25, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

The Army carried out the controlled disposal Wednesday near Middletown amid concerns about lead exposure.

Reuters and Associated Press reported Wednesday that the Army destroyed aging lead-containing explosives at its Iowa ammunition plant. The operation followed a court challenge and official warnings to nearby residents. Iowa health officials are coordinating environmental monitoring and advise residents to follow local safety instructions. Long-term environmental effects have not yet been established.

Sources: Reuters; Associated Press; Iowa Health and Human Services.
