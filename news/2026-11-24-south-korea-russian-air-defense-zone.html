SOUTH KOREA WATCHES RUSSIAN AIRCRAFT ACTIVITY

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Seoul says Russian aircraft entered its air defense identification zone.

Military officials clarified that the reported activity did not constitute a violation of sovereign airspace. The distinction matters because identification zones extend beyond national airspace.
