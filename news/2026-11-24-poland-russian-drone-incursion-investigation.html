POLAND INVESTIGATES DRONE INCURSION AS NATO TENSIONS RISE

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Polish authorities are investigating a reported Russian drone violation of national airspace.

The investigation follows earlier missile incidents and comes amid heightened concern over security along NATO’s eastern flank.
