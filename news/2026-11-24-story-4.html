HILL COUNTRY COLD SNAP! FIRST WIDESPREAD FREEZE POSSIBLE

Published Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

The Hill Country could see freezing temperatures on Wednesday and Thanksgiving mornings.

The Lower Colorado River Authority's holiday-week outlook identifies the Hill Country and Edwards Plateau as areas at risk of freezing temperatures. A little wintry precipitation is possible Tuesday, but significant effects are not expected. Temperatures should moderate over the weekend.

Source: Lower Colorado River Authority weather outlook, Nov. 23.
