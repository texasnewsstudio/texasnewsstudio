Why the European Union Wants Households Ready for 72 Hours

The European Commission has made household readiness part of its broader preparedness strategy.

Editorial context: Emergency readiness advice, scheduled alert tests and routine military exercises are separate activities. None of the cited official sources establishes that a specific global catastrophe is imminent.

A 72-hour goal

The EU’s March 2025 preparedness strategy proposes guidance for a minimum of 72 hours of household self-sufficiency. It includes cyber incidents, climate hazards, geopolitical security and other disruptions. EU policy.

Practical meaning

Families can build supplies gradually and adapt them to local hazards. The guidance does not announce that a specific crisis will happen on a particular date.
