Texas Border Mission Faces a New Legal Review

Operation Lone Star may face scrutiny under the incoming administration.

Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa has said she intends to review the legality of Operation Lone Star, Texas's expensive border-security initiative. That does not mean the mission has been terminated. The transition will test how state leaders balance border enforcement, budgets and the authority of federal immigration agencies.
