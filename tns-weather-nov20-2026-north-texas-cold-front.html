NORTH TEXAS TEMPERATURE CRASH! 70s GIVE WAY TO 30s

Dallas-Fort Worth faces a weekend cold front, a chilly Sunday and colder nights ahead.

North Texas is headed for a sharp weekend turnaround. The National Weather Service in Fort Worth forecasts highs in the lower to middle 70s Friday and Saturday before a cold front crosses the region late Saturday night into Sunday morning.

Behind the front, Sunday highs may struggle into only the upper 50s or lower 60s. By Sunday night, temperatures are expected to fall into the 30s in parts of North Texas. The below-normal pattern could persist through much of Thanksgiving week.

Some showers or thunderstorms are possible along the front, mainly north of Interstate 20 toward the Red River and perhaps east of Interstate 35. Forecasters currently expect generally limited rainfall, not a widespread severe-weather outbreak.

Patchy morning fog is also possible Friday, especially in rural areas, before improving by midmorning. Drivers should allow extra room if visibility briefly drops.
