Texas Hill Country Faces Chilly, Cloudy Monday Before Midweek Warmup

A shallow cold air mass is keeping South-Central Texas unusually cool, with some Hill Country communities expected to hover near or below 50 degrees Monday.

The National Weather Service's Austin/San Antonio office said in its early Monday forecast discussion that northeast breezes and persistent low clouds will limit daytime heating. The chill follows an unusually cold Sunday in Austin and San Antonio.

What to expect Monday and Tuesday

Cool, below-average temperatures are forecast to persist through Tuesday. The thickest low-level cloud cover may keep portions of the Hill Country especially chilly. Conditions will vary by community, so readers should check their local National Weather Service forecast before traveling.

When does Texas warm up?

Forecasters expect winds to turn more southeasterly as the week progresses. High temperatures across South-Central Texas are expected to recover into the 60s and 70s by Wednesday, with milder weather continuing through at least Saturday. Isolated showers become possible late in the week, but the weather service describes overall rain chances as low.

North Texas: Morning Mist Gives Way to a Gradual Warmup

Farther north, the Dallas/Fort Worth weather office expects lingering light rain, mist and patchy fog to end Monday morning. Tuesday remains cooler than normal before temperatures rise through midweek. Another cold front is possible next weekend, though timing and impacts may change.

Forecast sources: NWS Austin/San Antonio area forecast discussion and NWS Dallas/Fort Worth area forecast discussion, issued November 16, 2026. These links are source references, not substitutes for this original article.

November 17, 2026 update: Hill Country Warms Gradually After Chilly Tuesday Morning

The National Weather Service Austin/San Antonio discussion issued early Tuesday, November 17, says below-average temperatures persist through Tuesday. Clearer skies allowed some Hill Country locations to cool into the low 40s, with isolated rural low spots potentially reaching the upper 30s before sunrise. Daytime highs Tuesday and Wednesday are expected in the 60s to lower 70s across South Central Texas. A warming trend continues through the week, with low rain chances returning late Saturday.
