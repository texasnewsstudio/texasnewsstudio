H-E-B Salad Kits Recalled Over Undeclared Allergens

A Taylor Farms recall affected certain H-E-B salad kits distributed in Texas.

Texas shoppers are being alerted to a Taylor Farms recall involving certain salad kits sold through H-E-B. Reporting identifies undeclared allergens and a use-by date of October 30, 2026. Check the product details and official recall information; this is a recent recall follow-up, not a newly issued November 6 alert.
