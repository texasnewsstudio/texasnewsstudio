FORKLIFT BANDIT! THIEF HAULS OFF DALLAS ATM IN BRAZEN NIGHT RAID

Police are hunting a driver after a forklift was reportedly used to steal an ATM in Dallas.

TNS editorial alert artwork; not a photograph of this incident.

Texas News Studio | November 19, 2026

A Bank of Texas ATM on Walnut Hill Lane in Dallas was knocked over and hauled away with a forklift around 3:30 a.m. Wednesday, according to a November 19 Texas news roundup. A witness reportedly recorded the theft. Authorities were still looking for the driver when the incident was reported.

The striking detail is the equipment used: rather than attempt to break into the machine at the scene, the suspect apparently removed the entire ATM. The amount of money inside and the identity of the person responsible were not established in the report.
