Microsoft and Alphabet Earnings Put AI Spending Under the Microscope

Original editorial illustration by Texas News Studio; not a photograph of the reported event.

Investors are watching quarterly results from Microsoft and Alphabet scheduled after the market close on October 28, as attention focuses on spending to build artificial intelligence infrastructure.

Large technology companies are investing in data centers, computing equipment and supporting power systems. Investors are examining whether demand for AI services is translating into revenue quickly enough to support those costs.

For Texas, the trend intersects with the rapid expansion of data centers and the infrastructure needed to serve them. A strong or weak quarterly report does not, by itself, establish whether a proposed Texas facility will be built.

The key information will be the companies’ published results, capital spending disclosures and management guidance. This article was prepared before those earnings announcements.
