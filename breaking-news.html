OIL COULD REACH $120 A BARREL IF IRAN ATTACKS INTENSIFY

October 26, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

Oil prices remain elevated as traders assess the risk of renewed attacks on shipping and energy infrastructure in the Middle East. The possibility of further disruption to Gulf exports has kept markets on edge.

Reuters reported October 26 that Brent crude traded around $115 per barrel, while U.S. West Texas Intermediate traded near $101. The International Energy Agency also warned that global energy markets may not return to pre-war conditions in 2027.

Additional attacks involving Iran or strategic shipping lanes could raise transportation and insurance costs, constrain supply and put upward pressure on global crude prices. The $120 level is a potential scenario, not a confirmed future price.

Attribution note: A circulated claim attributes a roughly $120 oil-price scenario to Bank of America via Bloomberg. Texas News Studio has not independently confirmed the specific Bank of America forecast, so it is not presented here as a verified quotation or prediction from the bank.

Why it matters for Texas

Higher crude prices can benefit some oil producers while increasing fuel, freight and other costs for households and businesses. Actual gasoline prices depend on refining, taxes, regional supply and other factors.
