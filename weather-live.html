Thanksgiving storm threats stretch from coast to coast as Texas braces for cold

Holiday travelers face a mix of coastal flooding, strong winds, rain and snow across the country, while the Lone Star State prepares for another chilly morning.

TNS Weather Desk • Published November 25, 2026 • Editorial briefing: November 25, 2026

What you need to know

  • Atlantic coastal communities face a significant storm on Thanksgiving.
  • Snow threatens travel near the middle Mississippi River Valley.
  • Pacific Northwest mountain routes may encounter snow and wet conditions.
  • Central Texas faces a cold holiday morning, with a warmer trend later in the week.
