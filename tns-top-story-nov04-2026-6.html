WALL STREET WATCHES WASHINGTON! GRIDLOCK QUESTIONS GROW

Analysts assess how the congressional election results could affect markets and federal policy.

Investors are assessing the implications of Democratic control of Congress alongside a Republican White House, Reuters reports.

A divided government can complicate major tax and spending proposals, but the economic effects depend on the specific legislation and executive decisions that follow.

For households and businesses, inflation, borrowing costs and energy prices remain important practical measures to watch rather than assuming any single election result dictates market performance.
