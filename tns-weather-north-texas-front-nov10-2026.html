NORTH TEXAS NEARS 80 BEFORE COOL FRONT

Isolated showers Tuesday, warmth Wednesday, then cooler air Thursday.

The National Weather Service in Fort Worth gives much of North and Central Texas a 10–20 percent chance of isolated showers Tuesday. Temperatures could approach 80 degrees Wednesday before a cold front brings more seasonal weather Thursday.
