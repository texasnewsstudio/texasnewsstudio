AI PIONEER GEOFFREY HINTON WARNS OF LOSING CONTROL OF ADVANCED AI

Geoffrey Hinton warns that increasingly capable AI systems may become difficult for humans to control.

AI-generated editorial illustration; not a photograph of the reported event.

Computer scientist Geoffrey Hinton, whose work helped lay the foundations of modern artificial intelligence, is warning that developers may struggle to maintain control of future AI systems. His concerns focus on the rapid growth of autonomous capabilities and the challenge of ensuring powerful systems act in ways that protect human interests. Hinton has called for substantially more research into AI safety. His warnings describe potential future risks; they do not establish that current AI systems have taken control of society. The debate comes as AI tools become more widely used in workplaces, software development and decision-making.
