North Texas Rain Chances Surge Sunday as Temperatures Fall

Loading current conditions and forecast…

The National Weather Service in Fort Worth expects a dry, mild Friday before widespread rain arrives Sunday, with cooler temperatures accompanying the system.

The November 13 forecast discussion calls for Friday highs in the 60s and lower 70s across North and Central Texas. The weekend disturbance is expected to bring widespread rainfall Sunday and daytime highs mainly in the upper 40s and 50s.

The agency's Dallas forecast lists a 90 to 100 percent chance of precipitation during Sunday. Exact timing and rainfall totals may change, so travelers should review updated forecasts before departing.

The outlook is a forecast, not a report of rainfall already observed.
