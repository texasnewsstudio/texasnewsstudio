BORDER COUNTIES SHIFT AGAIN! TEXAS VOTE MAP REDRAWN

Democrats regain ground in South Texas counties that supported Trump in 2024.

The Associated Press reports a notable Democratic rebound in Texas border counties that had supported Donald Trump in the 2024 presidential election.

Shifts in voter coalitions can reflect local candidates, turnout, cost-of-living concerns and the issues emphasized in each election. They do not necessarily mean that every voter changed positions on immigration or border security.

For both parties, the results provide new evidence about the importance of Hispanic voters, younger voters and local economic concerns.
