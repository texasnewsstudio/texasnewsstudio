Hegseth Under Fire as Trump Defends Pentagon Chief Amid Questions Over His Future

Texas News Studio News Desk · November 5, 2026 · Reporting based on Reuters, ABC News and Forbes

WASHINGTON — Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth is facing new political pressure following Republicans’ midterm election losses, even as President Donald Trump publicly rejects suggestions that he plans to remove him.

Reuters reported Wednesday that advisers and people familiar with White House discussions have raised the possibility of a leadership change at the Pentagon. Trump, however, told reporters he believes Hegseth is doing a very good job. No dismissal or resignation has been announced.

Republican impeachment threat

Rep. Thomas Massie of Kentucky has threatened to revive impeachment efforts unless Hegseth resigns. Forbes reported early Thursday that Massie pointed to an anticipated vote when lawmakers return to Washington. An impeachment push is not the same as removal from office, and its outcome remains uncertain.

Iran war and congressional oversight

The war with Iran and its economic impact have become central to the post-election debate. Democrats, who won both chambers of Congress, are preparing greater oversight of Pentagon decisions. Questions about Hegseth’s handling of military leadership changes and the conflict could become major subjects of hearings.

What is confirmed — and what is not

Trump has publicly defended Hegseth. Pentagon spokesperson Sean Parnell says the defense secretary intends to continue serving. Reports that the White House has weighed options do not establish that Trump has decided to fire him. Texas News Studio will update this report if the White House or Pentagon announces a change.
