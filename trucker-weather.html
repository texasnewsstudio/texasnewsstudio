How the TNS Rating Works

The experimental 0–100 score starts at 100 and applies penalties for forecast wind gusts, precipitation, snowfall and potential freezing moisture. The daily rating reflects a weather-risk estimate, not a certified safety classification.

Data: Open-Meteo forecast API. Geographic display: OpenStreetMap / Leaflet. Scores and regional interpolation: TNS. Forecast availability, model updates and accuracy vary.

Planning information only. Forecast conditions can change rapidly. Road closures, traffic, crashes, construction, pavement conditions and bridge icing are not mapped. Never use this page as the sole basis for operating a commercial vehicle.
