Texas Homeowners Insurance: Why Premiums Can Rise

A look at weather exposure, rebuilding costs and the choices homeowners can compare.

October 27, 2026 · Texas News Studio

Editorial illustration; not a photograph of an actual facility or event.

Homeowners insurance prices depend on many factors, including the location and construction of a home, coverage limits, deductibles, claims experience and the cost of rebuilding. Severe weather risk can influence pricing across Texas.

A lower premium does not always mean a better deal. Policies can differ in wind and hail deductibles, replacement-cost provisions, exclusions and coverage for water damage.

Homeowners can review policy declarations, compare like-for-like quotes and consult the Texas Department of Insurance for consumer information. Specific premium increases should be attributed to verified filings or quotes.
