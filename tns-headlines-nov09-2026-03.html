NORTH TEXAS FACES COOL START, THEN MIDWEEK WARMUP

November 9, 2026 · Texas News Studio

Dallas–Fort Worth begins the week with cool mornings before warmer conditions and another front.

North Texas started Monday with temperatures in the 40s and 50s, according to CBS Texas. Forecasters expected a mostly quiet week, with afternoon temperatures rising toward the lower 80s by Veterans Day before a front brings cooler weather later in the week. This is a dated forecast, not a live weather warning.
