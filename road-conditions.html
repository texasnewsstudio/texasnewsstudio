How TNS scores highway weather

Each listed interstate is sampled at named locations. We use forecast daily maximum wind gusts, total precipitation, snowfall and minimum air temperature. Each sampled location receives a 0–100 weather score; the route's displayed score is the average of sampled scores, while its worst sampled location is highlighted separately. No score is displayed until live forecast data has loaded.

💨 Wind gusts

Stronger forecast gusts reduce the score. High-profile trucks can be vulnerable to crosswinds, which are not fully represented by a general wind forecast.

❄️ Freezing temperatures

Freezing air plus precipitation is treated as a possible icing hazard. This does not measure actual pavement temperature or confirmed ice.

🌧️ Rainfall

Higher forecast precipitation totals lower the score. Actual flooding and road closures must be verified separately.

🌨️ Snowfall

Forecast snow accumulation reduces the score. Snowplow operations and road treatment are not reflected.

👁️ Visibility

Visibility is not included in the route-wide rating. Check NWS advisories for fog, smoke, blowing dust and other reduced-visibility hazards.

📍 Route sampling

These are representative city forecasts, not continuous forecasts for every road segment, bridge, pass or interchange.
