Brazil Reelects Lula in Closely Watched Presidential Runoff

The outcome gives Lula another term and has implications beyond Brazil’s borders. The contest attracted attention across Latin America because it tested the strength of competing political movements and raised questions about Brazil’s relations with the United States.
