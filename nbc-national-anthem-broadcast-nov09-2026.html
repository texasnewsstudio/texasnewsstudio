NBC FOOTBALL BROADCAST OMITS NATIONAL ANTHEM

The anthem was reportedly not televised before Sunday Night Football; NBC's reason remains unclear.

The reason for the omission has not been established, and an absence from a television broadcast does not mean the anthem was not performed at the stadium.
