WHO CONTROLS THE TEXAS HOUSE? CLOSE RACES KEEP CAPITOL WATCHING

State legislative control remains a key question after a sweeping election night.

Texas’s election upheaval has reached statewide executive offices, but the composition of the state House remains important to the policy outlook.

Reuters reported that control of the Texas House was still undecided in its November 4 coverage. Legislative control will shape negotiations over taxes, schools and the state budget.

Because some contests can remain unresolved after election night, readers should rely on updated race calls and official canvasses rather than treating early margins as final.
