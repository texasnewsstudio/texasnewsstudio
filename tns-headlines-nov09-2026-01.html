SOUTH TEXAS FLOOD THREAT CONTINUES AFTER DAYS OF HEAVY RAIN

November 9, 2026 · Texas News Studio

San Antonio, Corpus Christi and surrounding communities face flooding concerns as saturated ground raises the risk.

Rainfall across southern Texas has left the ground saturated and streams running high. Forecasts published November 8 warned that additional storms could create dangerous localized flooding into Monday. San Antonio, Corpus Christi, Brownsville and Kerrville were among the communities monitored for flood risks. Residents should follow current National Weather Service alerts rather than relying on older watches or forecasts.
