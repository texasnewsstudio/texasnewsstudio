3.6 Million Ladders Recalled: What Shoppers Should Check

Ladders sold through major retailers are being recalled following reported head injuries and skull fractures.

The November 6 report describes a recall involving approximately 3.6 million ladders sold through retailers including Lowe’s, Costco and Amazon.

Consumers should check the exact brand, model and manufacturing identifiers against the official recall notice rather than assume that every ladder sold by those retailers is affected.

A fall from a ladder can cause serious injury. Anyone who suspects their ladder is included should stop using it until they have confirmed the manufacturer’s remedy.

The number of recalled units and reported injuries make this an important safety notice for homeowners, contractors and anyone preparing for seasonal maintenance.
