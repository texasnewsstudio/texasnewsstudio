NORTH TEXAS WARMS TOWARD 80 BEFORE LATE-WEEK COOL FRONT

November 10, 2026 · Texas News Studio

NWS forecasts isolated Tuesday showers, a warmer Wednesday and cooler weather from Thursday.

North and Central Texas could see a few isolated showers Tuesday, November 10, before temperatures climb toward 80 degrees in parts of the region on Wednesday, according to the National Weather Service in Fort Worth.

Forecasters place Tuesday afternoon rain chances at roughly 10 to 20 percent across much of the area. Widespread severe weather is not indicated in the early-morning forecast discussion, and the office does not list any watches, warnings or advisories.

A cold front is expected to bring temperatures back closer to seasonal averages from Thursday into the weekend. The precise placement of any additional rain later in the forecast remains uncertain.
