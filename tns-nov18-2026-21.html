TEXAS PILOT INDICTED AFTER DEADLY TUGBOAT COLLISION

Federal prosecutors bring charges tied to a 2024 Houston Ship Channel crash.

A federal grand jury has indicted a ship pilot following a deadly 2024 collision involving a tugboat in the Houston Ship Channel. Reporting cites a National Transportation Safety Board finding involving a lengthy personal phone call. The indictment is an allegation in a criminal case, not a conviction. The incident highlights the high stakes of navigating commercial vessels in one of the nation's busiest maritime corridors.
