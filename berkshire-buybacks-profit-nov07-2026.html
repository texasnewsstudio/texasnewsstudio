BERKSHIRE BUYS BACK $9.6 BILLION IN SHARES

The conglomerate accelerated repurchases even as quarterly operating profit declined.

Berkshire Hathaway said it bought back $9.6 billion of its shares in the third quarter. Operating profit fell about 6% to $12.71 billion, with weaker insurance underwriting results weighing on performance, Reuters reported November 7. Buybacks and insurance results offer two different windows into how the conglomerate is deploying capital and managing its businesses.
