TNS MASTER EDITORIAL RULE — 2026-10-27
Texas Local News is for county and city developments excluding earthquake news. All earthquake reports, including county-specific seismic events, belong exclusively under Earthquakes > Texas Earthquake News. Do not duplicate earthquake story cards in Texas Local News. Preserve earthquake standalone article URLs and archive entries.
County news requires verifiable, substantive developments; never generate filler for all 254 counties. Use correct county assignment, source attribution, original text, and dates. Preserve all old original pages, add new pages to News Archive and sitemap, and update the county directory only for counties with published stories.
