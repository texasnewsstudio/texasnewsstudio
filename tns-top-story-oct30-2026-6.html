ELEVEN GIGAWATTS IN THE PANHANDLE — BUT WHAT ABOUT THE WATER?

A colossal proposed Amarillo-area computing campus raises questions about a precious aquifer.

A proposed data-center development near Amarillo has become a flashpoint over water, energy and local transparency. The enormous Matador proposal envisioned an 11-gigawatt complex with its own power infrastructure, including plans for nuclear reactors. The region relies on the Ogallala aquifer, which has faced decades of depletion. The project has also experienced financial setbacks, and its original ambitions are not assured. Supporters see potential economic renewal, while opponents question water commitments and the way negotiations were conducted. For the Texas Panhandle, the debate is about much more than servers.
