STATE ATTORNEY GENERAL RACES RESHAPE LEGAL FIGHTS

NOVEMBER 8, 2026 · TNS NEWS DESK

A wave of Democratic victories, including in Texas, could change how states challenge federal policy.

Democratic candidates won several state attorney general contests in the midterms, including in Texas, changing the political lineup of offices that often lead major lawsuits.

Attorneys general can influence litigation involving immigration, consumer protection, environmental rules and other disputes. Incoming officeholders may review cases filed by their predecessors and set different enforcement priorities.

The election does not automatically end existing lawsuits or change the law. But it could alter which cases states choose to pursue and how they coordinate with the federal government.
