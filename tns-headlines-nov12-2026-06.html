U.S. Envoys Meet Putin Following Ukraine Talks

Diplomacy continues despite unresolved territorial disputes.

U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Vladimir Putin in Moscow after U.S.-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi. The discussions produced no major breakthrough, although negotiators agreed to pursue a prisoner exchange.
