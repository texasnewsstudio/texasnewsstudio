DEMOCRATS WIN BOTH CHAMBERS: WASHINGTON BRACES FOR DIVIDED GOVERNMENT

Democrats won control of the House and Senate, setting up a new confrontation with President Trump over legislation, oversight and federal spending.

Associated Press and Reuters project Democratic control of both chambers after voters shifted seats in several traditionally Republican states.

The incoming Congress will have greater power to scrutinize executive actions and shape federal spending, while President Donald Trump retains veto authority. The new balance takes effect when lawmakers are seated in January 2027.

For Texans, the practical questions include the budget, border policy, energy regulation and federal disaster funding. Election results establish who won; the policies that follow will depend on legislation, negotiations and court decisions.
