Pole Shift Videos: What Earth Science Actually Says

The idea of Earth’s magnetic poles shifting has an almost cinematic quality. But the science is more interesting—and more complicated—than the alarming videos often suggest.

Here is the situation

Earth’s magnetic poles wander over time, and geological records show magnetic reversals have occurred. Those processes are not the same as a sudden physical overturning of the planet or an imminent global catastrophe. Scientists monitor magnetic-field changes using observatories and satellites.

The bigger picture

TNS has not verified the specific video titled The Most Epic 14 Days of Science. Extraordinary predictions require dates, measurable evidence and credible scientific sources.
