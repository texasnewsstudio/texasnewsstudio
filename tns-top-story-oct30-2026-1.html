TEXAS PULLS THE PLUG ON DATA CENTER PERMIT MACHINE!

State officials disabled an automatic filing system after a clash over data-center approvals.

AI-generated editorial illustration. Not a photograph of the reported event.

Texas environmental regulators temporarily disabled an automated online filing system after watchdogs raised concerns about permits linked to data-center construction. Governor Greg Abbott had directed the state to pause relevant approvals while officials examine electricity and water needs. The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality said it suspended particular permits identified by critics. The move does not mean every data-center proposal in Texas has been cancelled. Other approvals and reviews may remain active. For neighbors near proposed facilities, that distinction matters: a temporary system shutdown is not the same as a permanent development ban.
