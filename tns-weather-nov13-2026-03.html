Texas Weekend Weather Shift: Rain and Colder Air Ahead

Loading current conditions and forecast…

After a relatively quiet Friday, parts of Texas are expected to see a notable change toward rain and cooler weather over the weekend.

The Fort Worth National Weather Service office projects a disturbance bringing widespread rain to North and Central Texas, with temperatures dropping well below Friday levels on Sunday.

The forecast highlights the importance of checking updated local guidance because the timing and intensity of rain can vary considerably from one Texas region to another.

The forecast applies to the Fort Worth office's coverage area and should not be interpreted as a statewide guarantee.
