Trump, Xi and Putin Prepare for Rare Nuclear Summit

A planned three-way meeting could test the future of arms control.

President Donald Trump is preparing for meetings in Asia, including planned discussions with China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin. The proposed trilateral talks are expected to emphasize nuclear stockpiles and strategic stability. The talks have not yet produced an agreement, and major disagreements remain over trade and security.
