Bose S1 Pro Speaker Battery Recall: What Owners Need to Know

Battery packs used in certain Bose portable speaker systems have been recalled over overheating concerns.

A November 6 consumer safety report says certain Bose S1 Pro System Battery Packs and S1 Pro+ System Battery Packs are subject to a recall.

The affected battery packs were sold through major retailers including Best Buy, Amazon and Walmart, but not every Bose speaker or battery is necessarily involved.

Owners should identify their exact battery pack and follow the manufacturer’s recall instructions rather than continue charging a potentially affected unit.

Lithium-ion batteries can present a serious fire hazard if defective. The notice is particularly relevant to musicians, event organizers and portable-audio owners.
