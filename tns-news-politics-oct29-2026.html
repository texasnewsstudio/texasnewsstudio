MIDTERM SHOWDOWN: CONTROL OF CONGRESS HANGS IN THE BALANCE

With Election Day approaching, Americans face a consequential choice over the direction of Washington. Republicans are defending their record and urging voters to focus on border enforcement, public safety and economic opportunity. Democrats are pressing their own case on affordability, healthcare and the administration’s decisions.

The stakes go beyond party labels. A shift in either chamber could reshape federal spending, congressional oversight and the president’s legislative agenda. National polling offers clues, but individual House districts and Senate contests will decide the outcome.

Conservative voters are asking whether Washington has delivered on promises to rein in costs and protect national security. Democrats argue that their candidates can offer a needed check on executive power. Both arguments deserve scrutiny against actual voting records and policy results.

The question for November 3 is straightforward: which candidates have earned the public’s trust—and which promises can be measured after the ballots are counted?
