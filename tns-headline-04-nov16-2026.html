Data Centers and the Hidden Cost of Texas Grid Upgrades

Who pays for new transmission capacity is becoming an urgent question.

Texas's growing electricity demand from large data centers is intensifying debate over grid-upgrade costs. Experts interviewed by the Houston Chronicle say developers should cover not just their direct connections but an appropriate share of wider infrastructure improvements. The rules remain contested, and household bills may be affected by the choices regulators make.

Why it matters

The development has implications beyond the immediate headline. The key questions are what has actually been decided, what remains uncertain, and how officials, companies or communities respond next.
