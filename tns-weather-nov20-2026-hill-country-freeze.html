THANKSGIVING FREEZE SHOCK! HILL COUNTRY COULD WAKE UP TO 32°

A Sunday front opens the door to chilly mornings — and a possible first freeze by Thanksgiving.

A warm Friday in the Texas Hill Country could be the calm before a much colder Thanksgiving week. The National Weather Service in Austin/San Antonio expects highs in the lower to middle 70s today, but a cold front moving through Sunday should begin a sharp change in the region's weather.

Forecasters expect breezy northwest winds Sunday, with daytime highs falling into the 50s and 60s for much of Monday through Thursday. The agency says Thanksgiving morning could bring the first freeze of the season in some locations, with lows in the 30s across much of South Central Texas.

For Horseshoe Bay, Marble Falls and nearby valleys, the main story is the possibility of patchy frost or freezing temperatures in colder pockets — not a guaranteed widespread freeze. Residents with sensitive plants may want to watch later forecasts, since the precise temperatures and timing could still change.

Rain chances remain limited in the latest forecast. The most notable shift is the colder air following an otherwise mild Friday and Saturday.
