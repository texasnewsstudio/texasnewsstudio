SPACE X, TEXAS AND THE NEXT BIG QUESTION ABOUT PUBLIC LAND

A proposed Boca Chica land deal is drawing attention beyond the launchpad.

TEXAS NEWS STUDIO · NEWS DESK

SpaceX’s growing footprint in South Texas is raising questions about the future of public land around Boca Chica. Reporting this week details a proposal involving Boca Chica State Park. The debate touches on access to the coast, wildlife habitat and the region’s expanding space industry. Any land transaction would need to be assessed through the applicable public process; a proposal is not a completed sale.
