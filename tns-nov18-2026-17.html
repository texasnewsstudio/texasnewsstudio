TEXAS ELECTION RESULTS FACE A NEW KIND OF AUDIT

The state begins its first statewide risk-limiting audit using paper ballots.

Texas election officials are conducting the state's first statewide risk-limiting audit of the November 3 election, according to reporting published November 17. The process checks a sample of paper ballots against reported results to provide statistical assurance about the outcome. An audit is a verification procedure; its existence alone does not establish wrongdoing.
