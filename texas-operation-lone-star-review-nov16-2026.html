Texas Governor-Elect Signals Review of Operation Lone Star Border Mission

Gina Hinojosa says she plans to review the legality of the multibillion-dollar Texas border initiative.

A November 16 Texas political briefing reported that Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa told the Washington Post she would review Operation Lone Star, while saying border security remains important. A review is not an announcement that the mission has ended. Decisions about funding, deployment and enforcement would require separate action. Texas communities and state agencies will be watching for details as the transition progresses.
