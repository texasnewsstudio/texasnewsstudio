Outages Put America's Critical Systems Under Scrutiny

Recent disruptions across banking and aviation have drawn renewed attention to the resilience of essential digital and communications systems. These incidents should not be described as a single coordinated attack without evidence.

Latest reporting

On November 16, customers reported problems with online banking and payments at several major institutions. Separately, the Federal Aviation Administration explained a November 13 nationwide ground stop involving unstable legacy network equipment during a routine router replacement. The agency said service was restored that evening.

Why it matters

The events highlight different kinds of operational vulnerability, including software defects, aging hardware and complex dependencies. They do not establish that the banking and aviation problems shared a cause.
