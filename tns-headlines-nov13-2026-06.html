A NEIGHBORHOOD DUCK SHOOTING VIDEO LEADS TO CHARGES

Texas game wardens say a social-media video helped identify two suspects in McKinney.

TEXAS NEWS STUDIO · NEWS DESK

Two people face wildlife-related charges after video appeared to show ducks being shot with an air rifle near a residential pond in McKinney. Game wardens say public tips helped investigators identify those involved. The case has raised concern among residents about both wildlife protection and safety near public spaces. The allegations remain subject to the legal process.
