U.S.-MEDIATED UKRAINE TALKS RESUME IN ABU DHABI

November 10, 2026 · Texas News Studio

Nearly four hours of negotiations end without an announced breakthrough; talks continue Wednesday.

Representatives of Russia, Ukraine and the United States met in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday for nearly four hours of talks aimed at finding a path toward ending Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

The meeting was the first three-way session since February. American envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner have been involved in efforts to narrow differences between the governments.

The biggest obstacles remain fundamental: Moscow seeks territorial and military concessions that Kyiv rejects, while Ukraine wants an immediate ceasefire and reliable security guarantees.

Negotiations are scheduled to continue Wednesday. The existence of talks is significant, but it should not be mistaken for an agreed ceasefire or a settlement. Fighting and long-range strikes have continued while diplomats meet.
