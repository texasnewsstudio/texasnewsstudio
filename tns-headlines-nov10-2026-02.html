U.S., RUSSIA AND UKRAINE RETURN TO PEACE TALKS IN ABU DHABI

November 10, 2026 · Texas News Studio

American-brokered three-way negotiations are set to resume in the United Arab Emirates.

Representatives of the United States, Russia and Ukraine are set to begin a new round of talks in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday, November 10, as negotiators revisit efforts to end the war.

The discussions follow several earlier rounds of diplomacy that failed to produce a lasting settlement. The renewed meeting is a diplomatic opening, not an announcement that a ceasefire or peace agreement has been reached.

The central question is whether the three sides can narrow their differences enough to produce a verifiable path toward ending hostilities. Any agreement would still require decisions by the governments involved.
