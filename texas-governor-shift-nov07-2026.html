TEXAS GOVERNOR RACE MARKS HISTORIC SHIFT

The state’s election result sets up major changes in Austin’s leadership.

Democrat Gina Hinojosa defeated Republican incumbent Greg Abbott in Texas’s 2026 gubernatorial election, according to Associated Press coverage. The result comes amid broader Democratic gains in statewide races and the Texas House. Attention now turns to transition planning and how the new leadership will approach legislation in Austin.
