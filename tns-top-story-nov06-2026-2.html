Meteor Showers And Supermoon Set Up November Sky Show

NASA highlights Taurid meteors, a close planetary pairing and a November supermoon for skywatchers.

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory highlights several November skywatching events. The Taurid meteor showers can produce bright fireballs, while a late-month supermoon and a Mars-Jupiter pairing offer additional reasons to look skyward. Visibility depends on local skies and timing.
