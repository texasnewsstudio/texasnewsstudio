NASA'S VOYAGER 1 REACHES A MIND-BENDING SPACE MILESTONE

The legendary spacecraft is expected to reach a full light-day's distance from Earth.

Voyager 1 is expected to reach a remarkable distance milestone on November 18: a full light-day from Earth, meaning a radio signal traveling at light speed would take roughly 24 hours to cross the distance one way. The spacecraft's decades-long journey puts the scale of the solar system into perspective. The milestone concerns distance, not a sudden new discovery or contact with extraterrestrial life.
