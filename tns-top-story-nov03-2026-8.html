MUSK EYES SPACEX–TESLA SHAKEUP! MEGA-MERGER TALK

Reuters reports a proposed combination aimed at greater control of Musk’s companies.

Elon Musk is pursuing greater control of his businesses through a proposed combination involving SpaceX and Tesla, according to Reuters’ November 3 news roundup. The possibility draws attention because both companies have enormous influence in aerospace, electric vehicles and technology.

A proposed deal is not a completed transaction. Any actual merger would require further details, review and formal corporate steps before its consequences could be assessed.
