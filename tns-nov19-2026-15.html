CRUISE SHIP SICKNESS SCARE! MYSTERY BUG HITS CARIBBEAN VOYAGE

Illness reports aboard the Caribbean Princess draw public-health attention.

Texas News Studio | November 19, 2026

A gastrointestinal illness outbreak was reported aboard the Caribbean Princess during a Caribbean voyage, according to a November 18 news account. The report says the ship notified the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's Vessel Sanitation Program.

The cause and final case count should be confirmed through the CDC's investigation. The existence of a reported outbreak does not, by itself, establish how the illness spread.
