Senfronia Thompson Announces Bid for Texas House Speaker

Veteran Texas lawmaker Senfronia Thompson has announced a run for speaker of the Texas House.

A November 6 report says longtime Texas legislator Senfronia Thompson is seeking the position of Texas House speaker.

The speaker controls important elements of House procedure, committee assignments and the pace at which legislation advances.

The announcement does not mean the speaker position has been decided. The outcome depends on the votes of House members and the legislative process.

The contest will help determine how the next Texas legislative session handles competing priorities following the 2026 elections.
