TNS TWO-PANEL WEATHER UPDATE

Homepage now displays only SPC Day 1 severe weather and live NEXRAD radar in compact expandable panels. The NWS temperature panel and MRMS rainfall panel were removed from the homepage to avoid repeated concurrent NWS observation requests and an indefinite connecting state. Breaking News Alerts remains below the weather panels. The original map remains in the weather page; no external weather navigation links added.

The weather map no longer polls station observations unless the temperature view is actually selected, and temperature requests are limited to 16 stations. Weather feeds remain external official services and must be browser-tested on the deployed host. Radar raster scan time cannot be independently verified.
