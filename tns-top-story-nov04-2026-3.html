WASHINGTON | NOVEMBER 4, 2026

CONGRESS FLIPS! DEMOCRATS TAKE HOUSE AND SENATE

Republicans confront a changed Washington after Democrats secure both chambers of Congress.

Democrats won control of the U.S. House and Senate in the November 3 midterm elections, Reuters reports, setting up a divided federal government for the remainder of President Donald Trump’s term.

Control of congressional committees, hearings and the legislative calendar will change hands. The White House retains executive authority, while Congress controls appropriations and writes federal legislation.

The practical questions now concern budgets, border enforcement, energy rules, oversight and the scope for bipartisan legislation.
