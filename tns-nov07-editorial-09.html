Ice Cream Recall Elevated Over Listeria Concerns: What Consumers Should Check

A reported ice cream recall has been elevated to the FDA’s most serious classification over possible Listeria contamination.

A November 7 consumer report says an ice cream recall was upgraded to the highest risk level because of Listeria concerns.

A high-risk recall classification signals potentially serious health consequences, but does not mean every product from a brand is affected.

Consumers should check the FDA notice for the exact product names, package sizes, lot numbers and distribution areas before discarding food or requesting a refund.

Listeria can be especially dangerous for pregnant people, older adults and those with weakened immune systems. The specific recall notice is the authoritative source for affected products.
