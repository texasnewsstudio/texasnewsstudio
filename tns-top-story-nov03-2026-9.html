UKRAINE ECONOMY UNDER SIEGE! STEEL AND GRAIN HIT

Russian attacks are disrupting factories, ports and power infrastructure.

The Associated Press reports that sustained Russian strikes are severely affecting Ukraine’s industrial output and export routes. Damage to steelmaking facilities, ports and energy systems has made it harder for businesses to operate and for farmers to ship grain.

The consequences reach beyond the battlefield: lost production and export earnings strain government finances and complicate reconstruction. The scale of damage continues to evolve as attacks and repairs proceed.
