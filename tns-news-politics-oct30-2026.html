ELECTION LAWSUITS INTENSIFY AS NOVEMBER 3 APPROACHES

Federal legal challenges in Alaska and California add new uncertainty to election administration.

Reuters reported October 30 that federal legal actions involving elections in Alaska and California have intensified days before the November 3 midterms. Alaska officials dispute a Justice Department lawsuit concerning certification, while Los Angeles County officials have criticized federal subpoenas relating to voting records. The claims remain contested, and court proceedings—not campaign assertions—will determine legal outcomes. More than 44 million Americans had already voted early, Reuters reported.
