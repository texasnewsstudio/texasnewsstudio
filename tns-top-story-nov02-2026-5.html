MILITARY WATCH | NOVEMBER 2, 2026

PENTAGON PLANS FOR 90-DAY CRISIS! BASES PREPARE FOR DISRUPTION

FORTRESS America aims to keep military installations operating when outside support fails.

The Pentagon’s FORTRESS America initiative focuses on the ability of military bases to operate for up to 90 days without outside support. Priorities include energy resilience, hardened installations, supplies and cybersecurity. The program is aimed at military readiness rather than a new civilian emergency order, but it underscores concerns about the effects of cyberattacks and other major disruptions.
