Dallas Man Arrested at JFK in Alleged $373 Million Bank Fraud Case

A Dallas-area arrest is linked to a federal bank fraud case involving 14 people, according to reporting.

A November 5 Texas news report says a Dallas man was arrested at John F. Kennedy International Airport in connection with an alleged $373 million bank fraud scheme.

The report says 13 other people were also indicted. An indictment is an allegation, not a finding of guilt, and defendants are presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
