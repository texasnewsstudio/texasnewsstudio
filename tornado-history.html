Data sources and limitations

The interactive map queries a publicly accessible ArcGIS archive described as SPC tornado paths for 1950–2020. It is not a live feed and may omit later finalized records. For more recent events, use NOAA's authoritative archive and Iowa State's storm-report and warning tools. Iowa State Local Storm Reports are preliminary reports, not verified tornado damage paths.
