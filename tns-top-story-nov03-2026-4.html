UKRAINE WAR | NOVEMBER 3, 2026

UKRAINE AID SITE DEVASTATED! RESCUERS SEARCH THE RUBBLE

The reported death toll from a strike in Zaporizhzhia rose to 31 on Tuesday.

The Associated Press reports that the death toll from a Russian strike on a humanitarian aid site and shelter in Ukraine’s Zaporizhzhia region has risen to 31. Dozens of people remained unaccounted for as emergency crews continued their search.

A wider barrage also struck several Ukrainian regions overnight, damaging infrastructure and disrupting power. The reported toll and operational details may change as rescue and assessment efforts continue.
