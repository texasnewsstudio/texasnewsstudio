Can Texas Freeze Electricity Bills? Governor-Elect's Promise Faces Regulatory Limits

Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa has proposed a residential electricity-rate freeze, but experts say Texas's deregulated retail electricity market limits the Public Utility Commission's authority.

The Houston Chronicle reported November 16 that the PUC oversees transmission and distribution charges, not the retail rates charged by most competitive electricity providers. Experts say the state may be able to scrutinize utility charges and change consumer protections, but an across-the-board immediate freeze would face legal and practical barriers. Data-center grid costs are another unresolved issue. For Texas households, the key distinction is between a political proposal and an enacted change to their bills.
