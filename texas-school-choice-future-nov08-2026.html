TEXAS SCHOOL CHOICE FACES UNCERTAIN FUTURE

NOVEMBER 8, 2026 · TNS NEWS DESK

The change in state leadership raises new questions about the voucher program and how schools will be funded.

Texas’s recently launched school voucher program faces a potentially significant overhaul after the midterm elections changed the balance of power in Austin. The program allows eligible families to use public funds toward private education.

Incoming Democratic leaders have signaled that they want to revisit the program. Supporters argue that school choice gives parents options; critics question the use of public money and the impact on traditional public schools.

Any repeal or major rewrite would require legislative action. Parents and schools should distinguish political promises from enacted changes as the new legislative session approaches.
