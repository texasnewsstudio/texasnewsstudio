HEGSETH FACES IMPEACHMENT PUSH AS PENTAGON BRIEFING LOOMS

November 10, 2026 · Texas News Studio

Rep. Thomas Massie has introduced impeachment articles against the defense secretary amid questions over the Iran war.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth is scheduled to face reporters at the Pentagon on Tuesday, November 10, as a new impeachment effort adds pressure to the administration's handling of the war against Iran.

Rep. Thomas Massie, a Kentucky Republican, introduced articles of impeachment Monday. The resolution raises objections to the secretary's conduct during the conflict and other actions. The measure's prospects remain uncertain, and filing articles does not itself remove a cabinet official.

The briefing is expected to draw attention because it would be Hegseth's first Pentagon news conference in approximately six months. Lawmakers and the public will be looking for answers about military operations and the administration's next steps.
