NO, A 90-DAY BLACKOUT IS NOT CONFIRMED! VIRAL CLAIMS EXAMINED

A military-readiness story sparks doomsday rumors; reporting does not establish a nationwide blackout plan.

Online claims about an imminent 90-day nationwide blackout have circulated alongside discussion of a U.S. military readiness initiative.

WBBJ reported on the viral claims and the actual context of the readiness discussion. Military preparedness exercises and warnings about possible disruptions are not proof that a nationwide blackout has been ordered or scheduled.

Readers should look for specific statements from utilities, emergency-management agencies and federal officials before sharing alarming claims. Practical household emergency planning is sensible without assuming the rumor is true.
