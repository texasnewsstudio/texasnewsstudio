TOXINS IN EVERY SAMPLE! SHOCK FINDING IN 212 PLANT-BASED FOODS

Researchers detect naturally occurring fungal toxins in every product tested — but the crucial safety detail changes the story.

Texas News Studio alert artwork — illustrative graphic, not a photograph of the discovery.

Texas News Studio | November 19, 2026

Researchers examining 212 plant-based foods and drinks sold in the United Kingdom detected at least one mycotoxin — a naturally occurring substance produced by certain fungi — in every sample.

The study covered meat alternatives and products such as oat, almond and soy drinks. The substances can enter ingredients such as grains, seeds and legumes during growing or storage.

There is an important qualification: concentrations in all tested products were below recommended European Union guideline levels. Detection alone does not establish that the products are unsafe or that consumers will become ill.

Scientists say the findings highlight a need to study long-term exposure across multiple foods and to improve monitoring as plant-based product ranges expand. The researchers did not demonstrate that the sampled products caused disease.

The study was led by researchers at the University of Parma with contributions from Cranfield University and published in Food Control.
