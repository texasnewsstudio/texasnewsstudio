Should AI Be Allowed to Choose Targets? UN Faces Divisive Debate

Diplomats are divided over binding rules for autonomous weapons.

A United Nations conference in Geneva is debating whether to negotiate legally binding limits on lethal autonomous weapons. Supporters of restrictions warn that systems capable of selecting and attacking targets raise accountability and humanitarian concerns. The United States and Russia oppose a binding pact, making a consensus outcome difficult.
