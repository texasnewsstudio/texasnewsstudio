SOUTH TEXAS FLOOD THREAT LINGERS INTO MONDAY

November 9, 2026 • TNS Weather Desk

Heavy rain and flooding concerns linger through Monday morning. Never drive across water-covered roads.

Heavy rain and thunderstorms have kept parts of South Texas on alert into Monday morning, November 9. The National Weather Service's national weather summary says excessive rainfall may cause flooding in southern Texas through the morning. The greatest danger is often on smaller roads, low-water crossings and locations where a short burst of rain overwhelms drainage. Conditions can change quickly from one neighborhood to another. Drivers should turn around rather than cross flooded pavement, and residents should check the latest local NWS alerts before traveling. This report summarizes a forecast, not confirmed damage at any specific location.

Official forecast source: National Weather Service / Weather Prediction Center.
