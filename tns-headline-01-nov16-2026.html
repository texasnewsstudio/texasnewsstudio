Can Texas Really Freeze Electricity Rates?

A campaign pledge meets the realities of Texas's deregulated power market.

Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa has proposed freezing residential electricity rates, but experts say the Public Utility Commission cannot simply set competitive retail prices. The agency regulates transmission and distribution utilities, while retail providers set many customers' energy rates. Consumer protection and the allocation of data-center grid costs are likely to become major policy questions.

Why it matters

The development has implications beyond the immediate headline. The key questions are what has actually been decided, what remains uncertain, and how officials, companies or communities respond next.
