How the TNS rating works

Our 0–100 planning score combines forecast high temperature, daily precipitation probability, wind gusts and cloud cover. Different activities have different tolerances. The score is a transparent planning aid, not a National Weather Service product or a safety guarantee.

Always check NWS alerts, air quality and lightning safety before outdoor activities. Forecast scores do not include lightning, UV, air quality or marine hazards.

Weather forecasts: Open-Meteo forecast API (live model forecasts; no key required). Geocoding: Open-Meteo. Map: OpenStreetMap contributors via Leaflet. Forecasts update as source models refresh. Displayed times are local to the selected location. If data cannot be retrieved, no score is shown.
