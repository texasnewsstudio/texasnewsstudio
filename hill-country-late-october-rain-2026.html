Hill Country Forecast: Warm Days Give Way to Late-Week Rain Chances

The Hill Country is enjoying relatively quiet October weather, but forecasters are watching for a wetter pattern toward Halloween.

Published October 25, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

AI-generated editorial illustration. Not a photograph of the reported event.

The Lower Colorado River Authority described a stretch of generally calm weather across Central Texas in its October 23 outlook. Overnight temperatures have been comfortable, while afternoon highs have remained largely in the lower and middle 80s.

The National Weather Service Austin/San Antonio office also expected mostly mild to warm conditions through the weekend. Its October 23 forecast discussion identified limited rain chances early in the week, followed by a more uncertain but potentially wetter period around Halloween.

For residents of Marble Falls, Horseshoe Bay and nearby communities, the main message is to enjoy the comfortable weather while checking updated forecasts before making outdoor plans later in the week. The timing and coverage of any rain can change as weather systems approach.
