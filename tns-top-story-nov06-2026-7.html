Millions Of French Presses Recalled After Burn Reports

Bodum customers are being urged to follow updated safety instructions involving metal utensils.

Consumer recall reporting describes a recall involving nearly six million Bodum French presses after burn reports. Consumers should check the exact product and remedy instructions through the manufacturer or official recall notice before taking action.
