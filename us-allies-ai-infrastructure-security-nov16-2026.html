U.S. and Nine Allies Move to Secure AI Infrastructure as China Competition Intensifies

Ten governments have agreed to coordinate protection of technology and infrastructure supporting AI supply chains.

Reuters reported November 16 that the United States, Australia, India, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Qatar, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and United Kingdom signed the declaration. The initiative underscores how semiconductor supplies, electricity, data centers and communications networks have become national-security issues. The declaration is a coordination commitment; specific implementation measures and costs were not detailed in the initial report.
