MYSTERIOUS 'POTATO CHIP' SHAPES ON MARS BAFFLE SCIENTISTS

NASA's Curiosity rover is examining strange crystal-like structures in Martian bedrock.

NASA's Curiosity rover is examining unusual disk-like and potato-chip-shaped features in Martian rock at a location nicknamed Nazca. Scientists are investigating what the structures are made of and how they formed, using the rover's onboard instruments and cameras. Their unusual appearance is fascinating, but it is not evidence of alien artifacts.
