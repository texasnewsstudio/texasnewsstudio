SCHOOL BUS SHOCK: MAN TRYING TO ENROLL RIDES WITH STUDENTS

La Joya ISD says a bus driver was fired after an unauthorized adult boarded.

A 37-year-old man boarded a La Joya ISD school bus with students and told police he wanted to enroll at Juarez-Lincoln High School, according to reporting summarized November 18. The district fired the bus driver, and the man received a jail sentence for trespassing. The unusual episode raises questions about student transportation safeguards, without establishing that students were physically harmed.

This original Texas News Studio summary is based on published reporting available for the November 18 edition. The underlying event may have occurred earlier, and developments may change.
