North Texas Faces Near-Record Midweek Warmth Before Cold Front

A brief spell of unusually warm November weather is forecast across North Texas before a cold front changes the pattern.

The National Weather Service in Fort Worth says Wednesday highs may reach the upper 70s to low 80s, near daily records. The front is expected Wednesday evening or night, bringing cooler conditions Thursday into the weekend. By Friday, highs should generally settle into the 60s, with lows in the 40s. A few isolated showers are possible before the warmer weather clears, but most locations remain dry.
