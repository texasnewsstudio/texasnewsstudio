Trump, Xi and Putin Prepare for Nuclear Talks Amid Global Security Strains

A planned three-way meeting in China puts nuclear arms control in focus while conflicts in Iran and Ukraine continue.

Associated Press reported November 16 that President Donald Trump's Asia trip is expected to conclude with talks involving Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. Officials described nuclear stockpiles as the focus rather than negotiations to end the war in Ukraine. The meeting is planned, not a completed agreement. Outcomes remain uncertain amid wider disputes over technology, trade and regional security.
