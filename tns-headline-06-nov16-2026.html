Ten Nations Join Forces to Protect AI Supply Chains

The new declaration puts chips and critical infrastructure in the spotlight.

The United States and nine partners have signed a declaration to coordinate security for technologies and infrastructure underpinning artificial intelligence. The countries include Japan, South Korea, India, Australia, Israel, Qatar, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and the United Kingdom. The initiative reflects growing concern about supply-chain resilience and strategic competition with China; implementation details have not been announced.
