HILL COUNTRY STARTS TUESDAY CLOUDY WITH PATCHY FOG

November 10, 2026 · Texas News Studio

Early NWS observations show low clouds and fog around Horseshoe Bay and nearby communities.

The Texas Hill Country began Tuesday, November 10, under cloudy skies, with patchy fog reported in parts of the Highland Lakes region.

A National Weather Service regional weather roundup at 4 a.m. CST listed Horseshoe Bay at 60 degrees with fog and calm winds. Llano and Burnet were also cloudy, with temperatures in the upper 50s.

These are early-morning observations rather than a full-day forecast. Drivers encountering fog should allow extra visibility and check current local conditions before traveling.
