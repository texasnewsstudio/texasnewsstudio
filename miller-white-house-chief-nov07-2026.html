TRUMP NAMES STEPHEN MILLER CHIEF OF STAFF

The White House leadership change puts a prominent policy adviser in a central management role.

President Donald Trump has named Stephen Miller to replace Susie Wiles as White House chief of staff, according to the Associated Press’s November 7 news roundup. The move places a longtime Trump adviser in charge of a pivotal White House coordinating role. The practical effects on administration priorities and staffing remain to be seen.
