Texas Storm Watch: Rain, Flooding Concerns and Cooler Weather

Published November 15, 2026 · Texas News Studio

Rain and thunderstorms are the main weather concerns for parts of Texas as a frontal system moves across the state.

Heavy downpours can cause localized street flooding, particularly in low-lying areas and places with poor drainage. Storm impacts can vary sharply over short distances, so statewide forecasts should not replace local alerts.

Drivers should avoid flooded roads, allow extra travel time and monitor National Weather Service warnings. Conditions can change quickly as thunderstorms develop or move east.

Reporting note: Forecast details are time-sensitive. Check the National Weather Service for current watches, warnings and updated local forecasts.
