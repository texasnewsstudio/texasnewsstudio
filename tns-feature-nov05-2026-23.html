The System Is Lying: How to Test Sweeping Claims

The phrase “the system is lying” is designed to make you stop and listen. It also raises an obvious question: which system, and what exactly is being concealed?

Here is the situation

Healthy skepticism involves checking documents, data, incentives and the possibility of error. It does not require assuming every institution is dishonest. The strongest investigations identify a precise claim, show the original evidence and explain what would disprove the conclusion.
