HOUSTON WATCHES VETERANS DAY SHOWERS AHEAD OF COLD FRONT

November 10, 2026 · Texas News Studio

Rain chances linger through Wednesday before drier air moves into Southeast Texas.

Southeast Texas faces a changeable stretch of weather as scattered showers remain possible through Veterans Day on Wednesday, November 11, especially near the Gulf Coast.

A weak cold front is expected to reach the Houston region late Wednesday and move through by Thursday, bringing drier air. Daytime highs are generally forecast to stay in the 70s through Saturday, with cooler overnight readings later in the week.

For outdoor ceremonies and travel, the timing of showers may matter more than the overall temperature. Residents should check updated National Weather Service forecasts before heading out, as rain chances later in the weekend remain uncertain.
