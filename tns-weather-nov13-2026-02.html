Pacific Northwest Faces Heavy Rain in Mid-November Outlook

Federal hazards guidance identifies heavy rain and precipitation in parts of the Pacific Northwest during November 13–15.

The U.S. hazards outlook highlights coastal Washington and Oregon and nearby areas for heavy precipitation during the period. Travelers in mountainous and coastal terrain should monitor local National Weather Service forecasts for more specific information.

Heavy rain can increase the risk of localized flooding and travel delays, but the outlook alone does not establish that flooding has occurred.
