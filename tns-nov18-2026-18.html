TECH OUTAGES | NOVEMBER 18, 2026

AIR TRAVEL, BANKS AND APPS FAIL — BUT WAS IT REALLY A CYBERATTACK?

A cascade of outages fueled speculation; officials say the incidents have not been linked.

A string of recent technology failures affected flights, banks, Microsoft services and major apps, creating a dramatic week for infrastructure that people depend on. Authorities and companies have not found evidence that the incidents were coordinated cyberattacks. Reported explanations include software problems, maintenance and aging systems. The outages are real; claims of a single hidden attacker remain unsupported.
