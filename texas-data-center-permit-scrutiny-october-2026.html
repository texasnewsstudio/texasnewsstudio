Texas Data Center Expansion Meets New Permit Scrutiny

State environmental permitting for data center construction has become a fresh point of scrutiny in Texas.

Published October 25, 2026 · Texas News Studio News Desk

AI-generated editorial illustration. Not a photograph of the reported event.

A Hood County Express October 24 news brief, citing other Texas reporting, said the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality had suspended initial stormwater and wastewater permits associated with 17 data center construction projects. One project reportedly involved a Google facility in Tarrant County.

The report said the permitting questions followed a September directive to halt certain data center permits. It also noted the agency distinguished automated notices from operational permits, an important detail when interpreting the status of individual projects.

The dispute highlights the tension between rapid data center investment and local concerns about land use, water, infrastructure and environmental oversight. Readers should not assume that every affected project has been permanently canceled; the regulatory status may continue to change.
