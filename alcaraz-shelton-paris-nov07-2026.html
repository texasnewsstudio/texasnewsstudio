ALCARAZ TO FACE SHELTON IN PARIS FINAL

A straight-sets semifinal win sets up a high-profile championship match.

Carlos Alcaraz defeated Casper Ruud 6–4, 6–4 on November 7 to reach the Paris Masters final. American Ben Shelton advanced by defeating Luca Van Assche in three sets. The final pairs two leading players with contrasting styles and a major title at stake.
