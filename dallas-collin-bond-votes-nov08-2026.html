DALLAS-AREA VOTERS REJECT MORE THAN $1 BILLION IN BONDS

NOVEMBER 8, 2026 · TNS NEWS DESK

Voters in Dallas and Collin County turned down major borrowing proposals, raising new questions about public projects.

North Texas voters delivered a striking verdict on local borrowing in the November election. According to a Dallas Express election roundup, Dallas and Collin County voters rejected bond proposals totaling more than $1.26 billion.

Dallas voters turned down a package of projects that included public-safety facilities. In Collin County, a roads-and-bridges measure also failed. Local officials must now decide whether to revise proposals, identify alternative funding or postpone some projects.

The results underline an important distinction: voters can support public services while questioning how much debt their local governments should take on. The next round of city and county budget discussions will reveal what changes.
