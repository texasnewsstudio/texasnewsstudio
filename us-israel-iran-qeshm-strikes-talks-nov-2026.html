Iran Reports New Qeshm Island Strikes as US–Iran Negotiations Continue

Iranian agencies reported projectiles striking Qeshm Island while Qatar said mediation efforts remain active.

Reporting note: Confirmed events and official warnings are distinguished from hypothetical scenarios and unverified attribution.

Iranian news agencies reported two projectiles striking the Qeshm Island area on Saturday, according to Reuters. At the time of the report, officials had not confirmed the extent of damage or released a complete account of responsibility.

The reports came amid a fragile effort to negotiate an end to the conflict involving Iran and the United States. Qatar publicly rejected claims that mediation had collapsed and said talks remained underway.

Earlier reporting described limited US strikes against Iranian missile installations and continuing warnings from both sides. Gulf states remain concerned about retaliation against energy and military infrastructure if the conflict escalates.

This is a developing story. Reports of an impact should not be treated as proof of a new large-scale offensive, and responsibility for the latest projectiles must be confirmed through further reporting.
