Texas Political Earthquake: James Talarico Defeats Ken Paxton in U.S. Senate Race

Texas News Studio News Desk | November 4, 2026 | Election-night result; vote totals may change as reporting continues

TEXAS — Democratic state Rep. James Talarico defeated Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton in the November 3, 2026, U.S. Senate election, according to race calls reported by the Associated Press, Reuters and The Texas Tribune. The result marks the first Democratic victory in a Texas U.S. Senate election since 1988.

The outcome was a major shift in a state where Republicans had dominated statewide elections for decades. Talarico's win also came during a national midterm election in which control of Congress changed hands.

How the contest unfolded

Paxton entered the general election after defeating incumbent Republican Sen. John Cornyn in the GOP primary runoff. He campaigned with President Donald Trump's backing. Talarico, a state lawmaker and former teacher, emphasized the cost of living and government accountability during his campaign. Paxton criticized Talarico's positions on social issues, while Talarico and his allies raised questions about Paxton's past controversies.

What changes now?

The result flips a Texas U.S. Senate seat from Republican to Democratic representation. The winner will serve a six-year term beginning with the new Congress. The contest also raises questions for both parties about their coalitions, turnout and campaign strategies in future statewide elections. Those questions are analysis, not additional election results.
