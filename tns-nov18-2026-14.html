SPACEX'S $24 BILLION TEXAS AI MEGAPROJECT SPARKS FRESH FIGHT

A proposed supercomputing complex near Brownsville is stirring debate over power, water and tax breaks.

SpaceX has confirmed plans for a $24 billion supercomputing data center and gas-fired power plant on a 444-acre site near the Port of Brownsville. The enormous proposal has also prompted questions about local regulatory control, infrastructure and proposed tax incentives. The company says the project would support advanced artificial intelligence applications, while public debate continues over how the development would affect the surrounding community.
