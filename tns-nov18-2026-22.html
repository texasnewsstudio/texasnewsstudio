CHIPS MISTAKEN FOR A GUN? STUDENTS SUE AI SECURITY COMPANY

A lawsuit alleges an automated school alert triggered a frightening police response.

Four current and former students at a Baltimore high school have sued AI gun-detection company Omnilert, alleging its system flagged a bag of chips as a firearm and prompted an armed police response. The students say officers ordered them to the ground and handcuffed them. These are allegations in a lawsuit, not established court findings. The case raises questions about false alarms and the use of automated threat detection in schools.
