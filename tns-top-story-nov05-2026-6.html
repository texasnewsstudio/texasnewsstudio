WALL STREET RALLIES AFTER MIDTERMS; INVESTORS WATCH GRIDLOCK

Stocks rallied and Treasury yields eased as markets evaluated the implications of a divided federal government.

Global equities rose after Democrats secured congressional control, according to Reuters market coverage. Investors are weighing the possibility of slower policy changes in Washington.

The reaction also reflects expectations for oil, inflation and interest rates. A single trading session cannot establish the direction of the economy or the stock market.

Texas businesses should watch energy prices, financing conditions and the cost of imported goods as the next policy debates unfold.
