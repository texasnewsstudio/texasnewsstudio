HALLOWEEN PARADE CANCELED! SCHOOL TELLS KIDS TO WEAR BLACK AND ORANGE INSTEAD

A Massachusetts school’s decision to replace a Halloween costume parade with a black-and-orange spirit day has sparked a familiar debate: how much should schools change long-standing traditions, and how much say should parents have?

For families who look forward to seeing children dressed as astronauts, superheroes and homemade cardboard robots, the parade is more than a photo opportunity. It is a small community tradition, and losing it can feel like another decision made without enough parental input.

Supporters of a simpler spirit day may see it as an easier way for every student to participate, including children whose families do not celebrate Halloween or cannot afford costumes. Critics argue that those concerns can be addressed without canceling the parade altogether.
