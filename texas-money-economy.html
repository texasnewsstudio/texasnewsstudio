How to read this dashboard

These figures are the latest published observations, not second-by-second market quotes. Some indicators are monthly, others weekly or daily. Each card displays its own observation date, so older figures are not mistaken for current-day readings. U.S. inflation and mortgage rates are national benchmarks, clearly labeled as such.

Data source: Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED), aggregating publications from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, Energy Information Administration, Federal Housing Finance Agency and Freddie Mac. Figures may be revised by the publishing agency.
