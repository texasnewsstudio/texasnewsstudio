CALIFORNIA DELUGE! RECORD RAIN FLOODS STREETS AS ATMOSPHERIC RIVER ROARS IN

A record-setting Bay Area soaking brings flooded roads, rescues and fresh Southern California concerns.

Branded weather alert illustration, not a photograph of the event.

Texas News Studio Weather Desk | November 19, 2026

A powerful atmospheric river slammed California's Bay Area Wednesday, producing record-breaking rainfall and flooding that stranded motorists and disrupted roads and services.

San Francisco recorded 2.11 inches of rain before noon, its highest comparable total in 76 years, according to reporting on the storm. Firefighters rescued motorists from flooded vehicles in San Jose, and the storm contributed to power outages and sewage overflows.

Forecasters warned the system could bring further heavy rain, coastal hazards and mudslide or debris-flow concerns as it shifted toward Southern California, especially around recent wildfire burn scars. Conditions and warnings are subject to change.
