PEARL HARBOR HERO COMES HOME — 85 YEARS LATER

A Navy sailor killed at Pearl Harbor is finally set for burial in Texas.

Kenneth Lynch, a U.S. Navy sailor who died in the 1941 attack on Pearl Harbor, is finally being laid to rest in Texas after decades. The homecoming marks a long-awaited moment for those honoring his service.
