HISTORIC STATEHOUSE GAINS EXTEND BEYOND TEXAS

Democratic gains in state governments could shape housing, education, healthcare and energy debates across the country.

The Associated Press reports that Democrats won additional governorships and legislative chambers in the 2026 midterms, including a takeover of the Texas House.

State-level control determines which policy proposals have a path forward on budgets, schools, infrastructure and regulation. Different states will pursue different agendas.

The longer-term significance will depend on whether the incoming leaders can translate electoral gains into durable legislation and public support.
