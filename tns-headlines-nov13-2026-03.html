TEXAS UFO FILES: WHAT DO THE STRANGEST REPORTS REALLY SHOW?

Old records describe unexplained lights and odd objects—but offer no proof of alien visitors.

TEXAS NEWS STUDIO · NEWS DESK

Texas has no shortage of UFO lore. Previously released government records describe unusual sightings near military facilities, including reports involving the Amarillo area and Central Texas. Some cases remain unresolved in the surviving documents; others have ordinary explanations or turned out to be hoaxes. The fascinating question is not whether aliens have been proven to visit Texas—they have not—but how investigators tried to sort fact from fiction.
