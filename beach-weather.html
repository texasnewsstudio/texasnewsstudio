Know before you go

Beach ratings are weather-comfort estimates, not swimming safety ratings. They do not verify rip currents, surf height, bacteria, jellyfish, beach closures or water temperatures. Check NWS rip-current safety, NWS marine forecasts, and Texas General Land Office resources before entering the water. Forecasts are from Open-Meteo, subject to availability and updates. No API key is required.
