INTELLIGENCE SHARING IN TURMOIL? OFFICIALS SOUND ALARM

Current and former officials describe disruptions in U.S. intelligence coordination.

Reuters reports that intelligence sharing between parts of the U.S. government and Congress has slowed or been interrupted during President Donald Trump’s second term, according to current and former officials. The report describes disputes among agencies and concerns about incomplete threat assessments.

Officials and observers differ on the implications of these changes. The account raises questions about how agencies coordinate during international crises, but it does not establish that a specific threat was missed.
