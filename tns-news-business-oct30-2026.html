TECH SHARES SLIDE AS WALL STREET QUESTIONS AI SPENDING

Investors scrutinize cash flow as major technology firms pour billions into AI.

U.S. technology stocks fell Friday, October 30, as investors questioned the returns on expensive artificial-intelligence infrastructure. Associated Press reported sharp pressure on Intel and Amazon shares, with concerns about capital spending and cash flow weighing on the broader market. These developments are particularly relevant to Texas, where data centers are increasing interest in electricity generation, transmission and water use. Market figures can change rapidly during trading.
