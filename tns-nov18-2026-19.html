TEXAS A&M CRACKS DOWN ON E-SCOOTERS ACROSS CAMPUS

New sidewalk restrictions and a speed limit are set to begin January 1.

Texas A&M University plans to ban electric scooter riding on main campus sidewalks and in parking lots beginning January 1, according to a November 18 news roundup. The policy also includes a 20-mile-per-hour limit and warnings that further restrictions could follow if unsafe behavior continues. Students who use scooters to cross campus will need to review the new rules.
