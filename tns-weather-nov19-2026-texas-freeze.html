TEXAS TEMPERATURE SHOCK! 30-DEGREE PLUNGE AS WINTER MAKES ITS MOVE

The Panhandle faces a sharp weekend drop, while colder air spreads south toward Thanksgiving.

Branded weather alert illustration, not a photograph of the event.

Texas News Studio Weather Desk | November 19, 2026

Texas is heading for a major weather change after several mild days. A strong cold front is forecast to push into the Panhandle Friday and Saturday, bringing a steep temperature decline.

Forecasters expect Dalhart and Amarillo to fall from Friday highs around the upper 50s to near 28°F by Saturday evening. Farther south, Dallas–Fort Worth should see cooler conditions through early next week, with highs trending into the 50s by Tuesday.

The National Weather Service says North and Central Texas have a 10–30% chance of showers late Saturday into Sunday, with many locations remaining dry. This is a forecast, not a report that freezing conditions have already reached every part of Texas.

Weather forecasts and warnings can change. For urgent decisions, follow the latest National Weather Service advisories.
