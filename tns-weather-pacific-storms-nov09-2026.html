PACIFIC STORM TRAIN TARGETS THE NORTHWEST

November 9, 2026 • TNS Weather Desk

Repeated fronts bring coastal wind, lowland rain and mountain snow to the Pacific Northwest this week.

The Pacific Northwest faces several rounds of unsettled weather this week as successive fronts approach from the Pacific. The National Weather Service Weather Prediction Center expects periods of rain in western Washington and Oregon, gusty coastal conditions and snow at higher elevations. A stronger system could bring heavier rain and mountain snow farther south into northern California and the Sierra by late weekend or early next week. Timing and amounts remain subject to change. Travelers should consult local forecasts and mountain-pass conditions before departing.

Official forecast source: National Weather Service / Weather Prediction Center.
