FLORIDA FLASH-FLOOD EMERGENCY RISK! RARE LEVEL 4 WARNING

Federal forecasters flag an extreme rainfall threat along Florida's Atlantic coast.

A dangerous rainstorm is threatening Florida's Atlantic coast Friday, with federal forecasters placing part of the area under a High Risk of excessive rainfall — the highest category on the Weather Prediction Center's four-level scale.

The National Weather Service Weather Prediction Center warns that low pressure near Florida's east coast will move slowly northward toward the Carolinas by Sunday. Heavy rain Friday could produce widespread, serious flash flooding, including in places that do not normally flood.

A Slight Risk of excessive rainfall extends to portions of the Southeast Coast on Saturday as the storm evolves. The forecast is a warning of potential flooding, not a claim that every location has already flooded.

People in affected areas should monitor local National Weather Service warnings and avoid driving through water-covered roads. Conditions and the precise locations of the heaviest rain can change rapidly.
